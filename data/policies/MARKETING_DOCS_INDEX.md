# MarketMind Marketing Document Index

Sample corpus for the **MarketMind** marketing knowledge assistant. All five documents describe the marketing team of the same fictional company, **Honeycrumb Bakehouse**: a scratch bakery and dessert brand founded in Portland, Oregon in 2011, with 64 cafés across Oregon, Washington and California, the Crumb Club loyalty program, celebration cakes, and the Honeycrumb at Home grocery line. Names, numbers, colors and rules are consistent across documents, so questions can be answered from more than one file.

The PDFs are generated from `scripts/marketing_docs_content.py` by running `python scripts/generate_marketing_docs.py`. Edit the content there (last-updated dates are in `LAST_UPDATED`) and regenerate; the search index rebuilds automatically on the next backend start.

## Key facts at a glance

- **Mission:** Bake everyday moments into celebrations. **Tagline:** "Life's sweeter by the crumb."
- **Palette:** Honey Gold #F5A623, Raspberry Swirl #E8456B, Cocoa Bean #4A2C2A, Pistachio #8DBF6A, Blueberry #5B5FC7, Vanilla Cream #FFF6EC.
- **Typography:** Fraunces (headlines), Nunito Sans (body), Caveat (one handwritten accent per asset).
- **2026 goals:** revenue $148M → $182M; Crumb Club 410,000 → 600,000 members; digital orders 28% → 35%; 12 new cafés (8 California, 4 Denver).
- **Budget:** $11.8M, led by paid social (26%) and in-café and local events (17%).
- **Big launches:** Summer Scoops gelato (June 15, 2026) and the Pumpkin Spice Croissant return (September 9, 2026).
- **Hero product:** the Honey Butter Croissant ($4.75; 2.1 million sold in 2025).
- **Competitors:** Sweetwell Patisserie, Daily Dough Co., Frostline Cupcakes.
