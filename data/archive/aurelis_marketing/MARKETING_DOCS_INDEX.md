# MarketMind Marketing Document Index

Sample corpus for the **MarketMind** Generative AI Marketing Knowledge Assistant. All five documents describe the marketing organization of the same fictional company, **Aurelis Technologies, Inc.**, and its customer intelligence platform **Aurelis Pulse**. Names, numbers, personas, campaigns and approval rules are kept the same across documents, so questions can be answered with content from more than one file.

| # | File | Owner | Focus |
|---|------|-------|-------|

## 1. BrandGuidelines.pdf
The source of truth for how Aurelis looks and sounds. It covers the company overview, mission, vision, brand positioning, the "Trusted Expert Guide" brand personality, tone of voice and writing style, logo use, the color palette (Aurelis Indigo, Pulse Coral, Signal Teal and the supporting neutrals), approved typography (Inter, Source Serif 4, JetBrains Mono), and channel rules for social media, the website and email. It ends with the content review process and a brand compliance checklist that every asset must pass before it is published.

## 2. MarketingStrategy2026.pdf
The CMO's annual marketing plan for 2026. It explains how marketing supports the company goal of growing ARR from $186M to $240M, and sets the targets: 18,000 MQLs, 5,400 SQLs and $310M in marketing-sourced pipeline. It covers target market analysis, customer segmentation, buyer personas, competitive analysis (InsightHive, Cortexa CX, DataLoom), a SWOT analysis, and the digital, social, SEO, paid advertising and email strategies. It also includes the $24.6M budget allocation, KPIs with owners, and a quarterly roadmap built around the 2026 campaigns.

## 3. CampaignPlaybook.pdf
The step-by-step guide to running a campaign at Aurelis. It covers the planning framework, campaign lifecycle and three-tier approval process, with an approval matrix showing approvers and SLAs. It also covers audience selection, the messaging framework, the content creation and creative review workflows, launch checklists, campaign monitoring, A/B testing standards, reporting, and how to run a retrospective. Worked example scenarios, including the Pulse Copilot Launch and Churn-Proof Summer, show the process applied to real campaigns.

## 4. ProductMarketingGuide.pdf
The product marketing reference for Aurelis Pulse and its modules: Insights, Predict, Engage, Connect, and the new generative-AI Pulse Copilot. It describes features, key benefits and competitive differentiation, with feature comparison tables. It also covers target customer profiles, messaging pillars, positioning statements, persona-specific messaging, elevator pitches, objection handling, sales enablement assets, customer success stories (Brightwater Financial, Northpeak Retail, Kestrel Health, Solace Software), the product launch strategy and the rules for talking about pricing.

## 5. MarketingFAQ.pdf
Forty detailed questions and answers for marketers, sales teams and agency partners. They are grouped into ten topics: brand guidelines, content marketing, social media, campaign management, email marketing, product marketing, customer personas, marketing KPIs, approvals and governance, and reporting. Each answer gives specific rules, numbers and owners, and points to the source document where more detail lives. This makes the FAQ a useful starting point for semantic search and question answering.
